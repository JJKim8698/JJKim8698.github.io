Tools I built

Running ads involves a lot of repetitive work around the campaigns. I design a tool for each recurring job and build it with Claude Code, usually in days, not months.

Stop paying for searches that never convert

Accounts leak budget on search terms nobody has time to review. Pulsemoon reads the search-terms export from Google Ads, finds the terms that spend without converting, and gives a negative-keyword list ready to paste back into the account.

On a sample report: 8 negative keywords found, USD 380 of non-converting spend flagged, in under a minute.

Pulsemoon · first version built in 1 day · reads files only, never changes the account

Know where every top-up dollar went

Top-ups, card fees and daily spend across many ad accounts usually live in a messy spreadsheet. Topmoon reads Google payment receipts and morning performance screenshots, records each top-up with the net amount after fees, and reconciles it with what was spent.

Built and put live in 1 day. I use it every morning for my own accounts.

Topmoon · web app on my own server · OCR for receipts and screenshots

Ad accounts and top-ups, without the back-and-forth

ADSMoon lets small businesses open Google Ads and Microsoft Ads accounts and top them up, with every fee shown before they pay. Behind it, the operations side connects several MCCs through the Google Ads API and imports each account's daily spend.

In production with customer sign-up, payments ledger and an operations dashboard.

ADSMoon · Google Ads API, multi-MCC · customer portal and operations side
